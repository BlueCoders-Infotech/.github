## ⚙️ Our Engineering Process

We eliminate unnecessary overhead to focus on speed, quality, and direct developer communication.

1.  **Product Discovery:** Aligning on your product vision, defining the roadmap, and mapping requirements.
2.  **UX/UI & Prototyping:** Designing interactive high-fidelity mockups in Figma to lock down user flows.
3.  **Development & Integration:** Developing clean, modular code with daily commits and weekly builds.
4.  **Testing & Launch:** Comprehensive cross-device QA, performance tuning, and App Store/Play Store launch coordination.
5.  **Support & Scaling:** Continuous monitoring, updates, and optimization to scale your product as your user base grows.
